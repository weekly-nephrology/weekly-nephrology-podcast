Today's words and phrases
  1. 1
    going in

    at the start, before something begins

    Example: Most of the team felt confident going in.

    going in は「始める時点で、入る前の段階で」という意味です。試験の参加者の背景を言うときにも、さらっと使えます。

  2. 2
    put it off

    to delay doing something until later

    Example: I keep meaning to answer that email, but I put it off again.

    put it off は「先延ばしにする」。登録や締め切りの話で、ついやっちゃうことを言うのにぴったりです。

  3. 3
    all the time

    very often, again and again

    Example: My phone buzzes all the time during clinic.

    all the time は「しょっちゅう、いつも」。よくあることを、気軽な口調で言いたいときに使えます。

  4. 4
    pile on

    to add more and more of something

    Example: Extra meetings can pile on stress at the end of the week.

    pile on は「どんどん上乗せする」。負担やリスクが積み重なるイメージで使います。

  5. 5
    by itself

    alone, without anything else

    Example: One odd result by itself isn't enough to change the plan.

    by itself は「それだけで」。一つの所見だけで判断しない、と言いたいときに便利です。

  6. 6
    part of the story

    one piece of the full explanation

    Example: The new data may be part of the story, but not all of it.

    part of the story は「話の一部」。全部は説明できないけど関わっていそう、と控えめに言うときに使えます。

Paper 1 · Dapagliflozin to prevent AKI after cardiac surgery, a randomized trial

Maya · Host
まず一本目。心臓手術のあとのAKIを、薬で予防できるかという試験です。
Leo · Co-host
In the Netherlands, 784 adults having planned heart surgery were randomized to dapagliflozin or placebo, starting the day before surgery, for just four doses. And AKI in the first week after surgery hit 28% with dapagliflozin versus 52% with placebo, using standard KDIGO creatinine and urine output rules.
Exact values

(Exact values: n = 784 randomized, 392 per group; dapagliflozin 10 mg once daily, 4 doses; AKI within 7 days 28% vs 52%; relative risk 0.54, 95% CI 0.45-0.65; P < .001.)

Of the three main uremic toxins they tracked, only TMAO fell more on the plant-heavy diet than on the control diet; indoxyl sulfate and p-cresyl sulfate didn't differ significantly between the diets.

This was a mechanism trial: 106 people with type 2 diabetes and CKD got weekly semaglutide or placebo for a year, to see how the drug protects the kidney. Here's the thing: the main MRI outcomes, kidney oxygen, blood flow and inflammation, didn't change significantly compared with placebo.

Paper 5 · Urine dipstick findings and UTI risk on canagliflozin, a post-hoc trial analysis

Maya · Host
最後の五本目は、外来でよくある悩みです。検尿で引っかかった人に、SGLT2阻害薬をどう考えるか。
Leo · Co-host
This post-hoc look at two canagliflozin trials in people with type 2 diabetes asked a practical question: does a baseline dipstick with leukocyte esterase or nitrite change UTI risk on the drug? Overall, canagliflozin didn't raise the risk of a first UTI or of total UTI episodes compared with placebo.
Exact values

(Exact values: first UTI HR 1.07, 95% CI 0.93-1.22; total UTI events HR 1.01, 95% CI 0.86-1.17.)

Maya · Host
2型糖尿病の試験参加者で、検尿の白血球エステラーゼや亜硝酸が陽性の人にカナグリフロジンで尿路感染が増えるかを見た二次解析なんです。 全体で見ると、カナグリフロジンで尿路感染が増えることはなかったんです。
Maya · Host
これは今年の九月にNDTに出た論文で、こちらも大阪大学の腎臓内科からです。
Maya · Host
じゃあ、検尿で異常がある人そのものは、感染しやすいんですか？
Leo · Co-host
In about 8,600 people, an abnormal dipstick was linked to more UTIs later, and the risk climbed as the leukocyte esterase reading got stronger.
Exact values

(Exact values: n = 8614; abnormal leukocyte esterase 10.7%; abnormal nitrite 3.5%; first UTI adjusted HR for leukocyte esterase 1+, 2+, 3+ vs normal 1.72, 1.92, 2.81.)

And in people with an abnormal dipstick, the drug didn't pile on extra UTI risk; the interaction tests weren't significant.

(Exact values: first UTI with abnormal leukocyte esterase HR 0.94, 95% CI 0.73-1.21, P-interaction .10; abnormal nitrite HR 0.92, 95% CI 0.59-1.43, P-interaction .45.)
