Today's words and phrases
  1. 1
    kicks off

    starts, usually an event or a season

    Example: The journal club kicks off again next week.

    kicks off は、イベントや企画が始まるときの、くだけた言い方です。学会や勉強会の案内でよく見かけます。

  2. 2
    safety-wise

    when we talk about safety

    Example: Safety-wise, the new schedule looked fine in the first month.

    語の後ろに wise を付けると、その点についていえば、という意味になります。cost-wise のように、ほかの語でも使えます。

  3. 3
    worth noting

    important enough to point out

    Example: It's worth noting that most patients came from one country.

    本題の後に、ひとつ添えておきたい注意点を出すときの前置きです。発表の質疑でも自然に使えます。

  4. 4
    a handful

    a small number, more than one or two but not many

    Example: Only a handful of residents came to the early lecture.

    少しだけ、数人くらい、という意味です。数字をはっきり言わずに、少なさを伝えたいときに便利です。

  5. 5
    undecided

    not yet able to make a choice

    Example: Many patients were still undecided after the first visit.

    まだ決めかねている、という状態を表す語です。外来で、患者さんの気持ちを説明するときにも使えます。

  6. 6
    ended up

    finally did something, often after other steps

    Example: We ended up changing the plan after the second meeting.

    いろいろあった末に、結果的にそうなった、というときの言い方です。研究の経過や患者さんの経過を話すときにも自然に使えます。

Paper 3 · Empagliflozin in ADPKD: a small pilot randomized trial

Maya · Host
三本目はADPKDです。SGLT2阻害薬がADPKDで安全に使えるのか、効くのかは、まだ決まっていなかったテーマです。
Leo · Co-host
This was a small pilot trial: 50 adults with ADPKD who were likely to progress fast, and weren't on tolvaptan, took empagliflozin or placebo for a year. Here's the surprise: kidney volume grew faster on empagliflozin, about 10.5% a year versus 5.1% on placebo.
Exact values

(Exact values: height-adjusted TKV growth: empagliflozin 10.5%/yr (95% CI 8.2 to 12.7) vs placebo 5.1%/yr (95% CI 3.0 to 7.3); P=0.001.)

Exact values

(Exact values: height-adjusted TKV growth: empagliflozin 10.5%/yr (95% CI 8.2 to 12.7) vs placebo 5.1%/yr (95% CI 3.0 to 7.3); P=0.001.)

Maya · Host
これは今年の十月にJASNに出た、アメリカのメリーランド大学医学部からの論文です。
Maya · Host

Leo · Co-host
Safety, the main goal, looked fine: side effects were similar between groups, and 96% in each group stayed on the full dose.

(Exact values: 96% in each group tolerated full dose; UTI: empagliflozin 2 vs placebo 0; AKI: placebo 1.)

Paper 4 · Upacicalcet for secondary hyperparathyroidism on hemodialysis: two placebo-controlled trials

Maya · Host
四本目は透析室の話で、二次性副甲状腺機能亢進症の静注薬です。
Leo · Co-host
These were two matching placebo-controlled trials in 768 hemodialysis patients with secondary hyperparathyroidism, testing upacicalcet, an IV calcimimetic given after dialysis. The main goal was cutting PTH by at least 30%, judged near the end of the 27-week treatment. In both trials, over 40% of upacicalcet patients reached that PTH goal, compared with about 17% on placebo.
Exact values

(Exact values: PATH-1: 42.1% vs 16.8%; difference 25.3% (95% CI 16.2 to 34.5); P<0.001; PATH-2: 43.5% vs 17.4%; difference 26.1% (95% CI 17.4 to 34.8); P<0.001.)

Maya · Host

Exact values

(Exact values: PATH-1: 42.1% vs 16.8%; difference 25.3% (95% CI 16.2 to 34.5); P<0.001; PATH-2: 43.5% vs 17.4%; difference 26.1% (95% CI 17.4 to 34.8); P<0.001.)

(Exact values: corrected Ca <7.5 mg/dl: PATH-1 31% vs 12%; PATH-2 45% vs 19%; dose interruptions: PATH-1 44% vs 24%; PATH-2 52% vs 27%; symptomatic hypocalcemia (investigator-attributed): upacicalcet 0.8% (PATH-1) and 2% (PATH-2); placebo none.)

(Exact values: ACP documentation at 12 months: 56.6% vs 40.0%; rate difference 16.6% (95% CI 7.5% to 25.7%); P<.001; documented discussion of treatment options: 46.1% vs 31.6%; rate difference 14.5% (95% CI 5.6% to 23.4%); P=.001.)
