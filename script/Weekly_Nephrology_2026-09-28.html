Today's words and phrases
  1. 1
    picked up

    Found or noticed something.

    Example: The night nurse picked up the change in his breathing.

    picked up は「拾い上げた、気づいた」。検査や観察で何かを見つけたときに使えます。

  2. 2
    came off

    Stopped needing or using something.

    Example: She came off the ventilator after a few days.

    came off は「〜から外れた、やめた」。人工呼吸器や透析から離脱した、という場面でよく使います。

  3. 3
    in the ballpark

    Close to a rough estimate.

    Example: My guess for the waiting time was in the ballpark.

    in the ballpark は「だいたいその範囲」。ざっくりした見積もりが合っているときの言い方です。

  4. 4
    fall through the cracks

    Be missed because nobody takes responsibility.

    Example: Follow-up results can fall through the cracks after discharge.

    fall through the cracks は「見落とされる、こぼれ落ちる」。担当のすき間で抜ける、というニュアンスです。

  5. 5
    rule of thumb

    A simple practical guide, not an exact rule.

    Example: My rule of thumb is to read the label before every dose.

    rule of thumb は「目安、経験則」。厳密な決まりではない、というところがポイントです。

  6. 6
    early days

    Too soon to be sure how things will turn out.

    Example: It's early days, but the new clinic seems to be working.

    early days は「まだ始まったばかり、結論は早い」。期待はあるけど慎重に、というときにぴったりです。
