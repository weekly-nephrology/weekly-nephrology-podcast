Today's words and phrases
  1. 1
    on file

    kept in the records, so you can look it up later

    Example: There was no recent urine result on file for him.

    「記録にある」ってことです。カルテに結果があるかどうかを言うときに使えます。

  2. 2
    in random order

    in an order decided by chance, not by anyone's choice

    Example: Each volunteer tried both diets in random order.

    「順番はくじ引きで」。クロスオーバー試験の説明でよく出ます。

  3. 3
    stayed below zero

    kept under zero the whole time, never going above it

    Example: The change in weight stayed below zero at every visit.

    「ずっとマイナス側だった」。変化の向きがぶれなかった、と言うときに。

  4. 4
    pitch it as

    to present something in a certain way, so people see it like that

    Example: The speaker tried to pitch it as a small first step.

    「〜として打ち出す」。学会発表の話でも使えます。

  5. 5
    leaves room for

    still allows something else to be possible

    Example: The small sample leaves room for a different answer.

    「〜の余地を残す」。結論を急がないときの言い回しです。

  6. 6
    left out

    not included, often on purpose

    Example: We left out anyone who moved away during the study.

    「外した」。exclude より口語的で使いやすいです。
