Today's words and phrases
  1. 1
    ease into

    to start something slowly and gently

    Example: She likes to ease into the week with a short meeting on Monday.

    ease into は「少しずつ慣らしながら始める」で、新しい仕事やリズムに入るときに使えます。

  2. 2
    show up

    to appear or become visible

    Example: The change did not show up until the second month.

    show up は「人が来る」だけでなく、「結果や変化が見えてくる」にも使えて、データの話で便利です。

  3. 3
    keep an eye on

    to watch something carefully over time

    Example: The team will keep an eye on the new schedule this month.

    keep an eye on は「目を離さずに見ておく」で、経過を見守るニュアンスです。

  4. 4
    treatment burden

    the effort and stress that a treatment puts on a patient

    Example: The doctor asked about treatment burden before adding another pill.

    treatment burden は「治療の負担」で、患者さんが治療に感じる重さを指します。患者さん目線の研究でよく出てきます。

  5. 5
    on paper

    in theory, but not yet tested in real life

    Example: The plan looks good on paper, but we need to try it.

    on paper は「理屈の上では」で、実際にやってみないと分からない、という含みがあります。

  6. 6
    the big picture

    the main overall situation, not the small details

    Example: Before the details, let me give you the big picture.

    the big picture は「全体像」で、細かい話の前に大づかみに話すときの決まり文句です。
