  2. 2

    collected different things and joined them into one whole

  3. 3

    to work about as well as something else

  4. 4

    not easy to find, buy, or reach

    手に入りにくい、という意味です。薬や検査が地域によって使いにくい事情を話すときにも、自然に使えます。

  5. 5
    tempting

    attractive in a way that makes you want to accept it, sometimes too quickly

Paper 1 · Network meta-analysis of MRAs for albuminuria in CKD
