Today's words and phrases
  1. 1
    sizing up

    Judging or estimating something, often quickly

    Example: She spent a minute sizing up the new patient before asking questions

    sizing up は「品定めする」「見積もる」という感じです。相手や状況をざっと見て判断するときに使えます。

  2. 2
    back up

    To go back to an earlier point in a story or discussion

    Example: Let's back up and look at what happened last week

    back up は、ここでは「話を少し戻す」という意味です。会議や症例検討で時系列を戻すときに便利です。

  3. 3
    a wave of

    A sudden large amount of something arriving together

    Example: The clinic had a wave of flu cases in January

    a wave of は「どっと押し寄せる」感じを出す言い方です。患者さんや問い合わせが一度に増えたときに使えます。

  4. 4
    in the maybe box

    Not accepted or rejected yet, kept as a possibility

    Example: I'll put that idea in the maybe box until we see more data

    in the maybe box は「保留の箱に入れておく」という言い方です。採用も却下もまだ、というときにぴったりです。

  5. 5
    most likely trigger

    The cause that seems most probable to have started something

    Example: Stress was the most likely trigger for his headache

    most likely trigger は「いちばん疑わしいきっかけ」です。因果を断定せずに言いたいときに便利です。

  6. 6
    urine microscopy

    Looking at a urine sample under a microscope

    Example: Urine microscopy showed a few red cells

    urine microscopy は「尿沈渣の鏡検」です。英語の論文や学会では、この言い方でよく出てきます。
